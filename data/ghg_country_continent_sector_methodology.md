# Country source detail and continental sector comparison, 2025

Source: EDGAR 2026, EDGAR_AR5_GHG_1970_2025.xlsx, IPCC 2006.
https://edgar.jrc.ec.europa.eu/dataset_ghg2026
Sum fossil and bio rows by source/country. Gg divided by 1000 gives Mt CO2eq.
All country source sums reconcile to TOTALS BY COUNTRY to relative tolerance 1e-8.
World reproduces the previous 38-source global treemap exactly, including AIR/SEA
international transport and excluding historical ANT/SCG groupings. Country
options comprise 220 current country/territory records, excluding AIR and SEA.
Switching country resets category expansion and recomputes percentages, category
totals and the leading source. Source definitions and five major groups are
unchanged. Finland contains a -0.265640375 Mt solid-fuel source adjustment:
the original value remains in net/category totals and shares; its negative area
is not drawn and a visible note explains this exception. No source is invented.

Continental heatmap aggregates the same mutually exclusive eight-sector mapping
as the former country heatmap. Each share equals summed sector emissions divided
by the sum of all eight sector emissions in that continent, not the unweighted
mean of country percentages. Each row sums to 100%. Country source totals and all
ten previous country-sector records reconcile to the unchanged original data.
Natural Earth 1:10m country ISO/administrative codes supply continent assignments;
Russia is Europe. GLP/MTQ, GUF, MYT/REU and TKL are not separate Natural Earth
country records; their continental assignments follow their EDGAR geographic
groups: Rest Central America, Rest South America, Eastern Africa and Oceania.
Natural Earth groups Maldives, Mauritius, Saint Helena and Seychelles under Seven seas; their EDGAR geographic groups place Maldives in Asia and the other three in Africa. All 220 country/territory records are included once; continent country counts sum to 220 and continental emissions reconcile to the sum of all national totals. International aviation and
shipping and LULUCF are excluded from continental totals. The global treemap
includes international transport, so it has a different denominator.
