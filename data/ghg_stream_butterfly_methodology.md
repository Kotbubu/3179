# Sector history and Australian peer comparison

The 1970–2025 streamgraph sums the same eight mutually exclusive IPCC 2006
sector groups as the former lollipop. Fossil and bio rows are summed. AIR/SEA
international transport is included; ANT/SCG historical aggregates and LULUCF
are excluded. Every annual sum reconciles with TOTALS BY COUNTRY, using the
same exclusions, to relative tolerance 1e-10. The 1990 and 2025 sector values
match the former lollipop exactly. Band order is fixed and centre-stacked.
Only thickness encodes quantity; vertical placement does not encode emissions.
Highlighting changes opacity only, never data or stack positions.

The butterfly compares 2025 Australian emissions with the 76 other high-income
countries in the existing 203-country emissions/population join. Each peer
sector rate is summed sector emissions divided by summed population (1,372,104,661),
not a mean of country rates. Australia is excluded from the peers. The five
major categories follow the existing treemap. Australian sector rates sum to
the national per-person rate. The peer total reconciles with the joined national
emissions. Both scales use 0–16 tonnes CO2eq/person. The source negative adjustment
in Finland is retained in aggregate Energy emissions, which remain positive.
Income classifications are the downloaded metadata snapshot, not historical ones.

The national trend annotation uses the observed 2000 US peak of 7,176.8767605639 Mt.
Chinese annual emissions increased by 14,293.4596619138 Mt between 1970 and 2025.
US and Japanese 2025 values are 16.143% and 25.163% below their respective peaks.
