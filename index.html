Reading the numbers

What is CO₂eq?

Greenhouse gases trap different amounts of heat.CO₂eq means carbon dioxide equivalent.It puts their warming effects on one scale.This comparison uses a 100-year time horizon.

Total or per person?

1 Mt CO₂eq = one million tonnes CO₂ equivalent.Total emissions describe a whole country.Per-person emissions divide that total by population.They are measured in tonnes CO₂eq per person.
