THE PICTURE TO TAKE AWAY

One footprint, several perspectives

Population, income, history and sector mix change the comparison.
Australia shows why national totals alone cannot tell the story.

People and emissions

The ten largest emitters contribute 67.8% of covered emissions.
They account for 50.8% of the covered population.

Sources and change

Power and heat produce about 29% of global emissions.
Their annual emissions rose by 11,951 Mt CO₂eq
between 1970 and 2025.

Australia in focus

Per-person emissions fell while national totals grew since 1970.
Local hotspots reveal another layer of concentration.

Sources and methodology

Data sources

EDGAR 2026: national, sector and gridded emissions.
World Bank SP.POP.TOTL: population and income metadata.
Natural Earth: boundaries and country label locations.

Coverage and comparison

Land use and forestry are excluded.
Global sector totals include international transport; national charts exclude it.
Population comparisons match country codes and years.
Peer rates pool emissions and population, excluding Australia.
